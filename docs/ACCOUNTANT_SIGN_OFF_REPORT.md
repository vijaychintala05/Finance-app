## 1. Executive Summary

As part of the **Stage 8 Production Qualification and Controlled Rollout Gate**, the FirmBooks financial management core underwent automated and manual accounting qualification audits.

The evaluation verified that:
1. Every monetary transaction adheres strictly to double-entry bookkeeping (`Debits == Credits`).
2. Subledgers for Accounts Receivable (`1100`), Accounts Payable (`2000`), and Cash/Bank accounts (`1000`) reconcile to their general ledger control accounts with **zero drift**.
3. All balance sheet and profit & loss statements satisfy the fundamental accounting equation:
   $$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity} + \text{Net Profit}$$
4. Temporal period locks prevent backdated transactions and retroactive edits to closed financial periods.
5. Multi-currency and decimal calculations are protected against IEEE 754 floating-point errors using cent/integer scaling and banker's rounding algorithms.

Based on these results, **FirmBooks is hereby certified as mathematically sound and approved for production accounting operations.**

## 3. Scope of Verification

### 3.1 Order-to-Cash (O2C) Lifecycle
- **Commercial Rounding & GST**: Invoice tax line calculations and discount distributions reconcile to the cent with automated remainder distribution.
- **Receivable Control Parity**: Posted invoices post immediate debits to `1100 Accounts Receivable` and credits to revenue and GST payable. Unpaid invoice balances exactly equal the balance on `1100`.
- **Payment Allocation**: Customer payments credit `1100` and debit `1000 Operating Bank Account`, updating invoice payment status from `Draft`/`Posted` to `Partially Paid` and `Paid`.

### 3.2 Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Lifecycle
- **Payables Accrual**: Vendor bills credit `2000 Accounts Payable` and debit corresponding expense / asset accounts.
- **Disbursement Reconciliation**: Payments made reduce `2000 Accounts Payable` and credit `1000 Operating Bank Account`. Subledger aging reports reconcile directly to general ledger balances.

### 3.3 Banking & Cash Management
- **Transaction Verification**: Bank statement reconciliation verifies statement balances against internal cash account ledgers.
- **Unreconciled Item Tracking**: Strict separation of reconciled, matched, and pending bank transactions with audit tracking.

### 3.4 Period Close & Immutability
- **Period Locks**: The `period_locks` mechanism blocks any insertion, update, or deletion of journal entries, invoices, bills, or payments with a transaction date on or before the lock date.
- **Tenant Recovery Locks**: If a tenant restoration or disaster recovery drill is active, the organization is locked into read-only mode to prevent partial writes.
